Version 1.0 is the first release of Tigroeb, a points league for friends who share one phone.

• Week board as home, with Fixtures, Standings, and Settings as sheets
• 4 by 4 lattice from 0 0 through 3 3
• Aim a cell, Seal it before kickoff, Peel it before kickoff, Post the final goals after kickoff
• Skew when a second Aim is taken without Peel
• Void when kickoff day arrives while the line is still OPEN or AIMED
• Points: bullseye 9, near 3, flat 0
• Add a fixture with Home side, Away side, and Kickoff daykey
• Switch among existing tipsters and rename them
• Reset this round and Reset all data
• Replay onboarding
• Contact
• Burst on a bullseye
• Light haptic on a successful Aim, Seal, Post, or Peel

There is no account and no purse. Data stays on this device.
