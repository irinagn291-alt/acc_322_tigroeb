Tigroeb is a points league for friends who share one phone. Each tipster aims an exact match score on a 4 by 4 lattice, seals that cell before kickoff, then posts the final goals so the standings can award a bullseye, a near, or a flat.

• Week board with a fixture carousel that never leaves home
• 4 by 4 lattice of exact scores from 0 0 through 3 3
• Aim a cell, Seal it before kickoff, Peel it before kickoff, Post the final goals after kickoff
• A second Aim without Peel keeps the first cell and shows A skew was recorded.
• Void when kickoff day arrives while the line is still OPEN or AIMED
• Points: bullseye 9, near 3, flat 0, shown on POSTED POINTS and Standings
• One posted final per fixture, scored for every tipster who sealed
• Add a fixture with Home side, Away side, and Kickoff daykey
• Switch among existing tipsters and rename them
• Reset this round and Reset all data
• Replay onboarding
• Contact
• Burst on a bullseye

There is no account and no purse; only points on this device.
